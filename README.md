# GrowthPilot | Google Ads & Growth Marketing
**Qualified Leads. Smarter Growth. Measurable Results.**

GrowthPilot helps businesses grow through data-driven Google Ads campaigns, conversion-focused landing pages, conversion rate optimization (CRO), analytics, and AI-powered automation.

Our focus is simple: turn high-intent searches into meaningful business opportunities—not just clicks and impressions.

## 🚀 About GrowthPilot
GrowthPilot is a performance marketing brand focused on helping service-based businesses attract qualified prospects, generate more enquiries, and improve marketing efficiency.

We combine paid search strategy, landing page optimization, conversion tracking, and data analysis to build measurable growth systems.

## 💼 Our Services
- **Google Ads Management** — Search campaign strategy, keyword research, ad copy, bidding, and optimization.
- **Lead Generation** — Campaigns designed to generate qualified enquiries and calls.
- **Landing Page Design** — Conversion-focused pages aligned with search intent and advertising messages.
- **Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)** — Identify and improve the factors that affect lead conversion.
- **Analytics & Tracking** — Google Ads conversion tracking, GA4, Google Tag Manager, and performance reporting.
- **AI Automation** — Explore practical automation opportunities that improve marketing workflows and efficiency.

## 🎯 Our Mission
To help businesses make smarter marketing decisions, reach high-intent customers, and turn advertising investment into measurable business growth.

## 🧭 Our Approach
1. **Discover** — Understand the business, audience, offer, and growth objectives.
2. **Research** — Analyze search intent, keywords, competition, and customer needs.
3. **Build** — Develop campaigns, landing pages, and conversion tracking.
4. **Launch** — Deploy campaigns with clearly defined goals and measurement.
5. **Optimize** — Evaluate search terms, costs, conversion rates, and lead quality.
6. **Scale** — Identify opportunities to grow based on performance data.
